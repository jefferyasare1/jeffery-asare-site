Order Workflow

Every step from sale to retention, in order.

1
Customer buys

Buyer pays via Paystack. Confirmation email fires automatically. You get an alert email. COA appears on screen. Add the order to your dashboard here.

Automatic · then add to Orders tab

2
Production: 7 to 14 business days

Send file to printer. Inspect quality. Sign and number the print. Package in acid-free tissue with rigid backing.

Your action: physical

3
Ship and send tracking

Hand to DHL. Use the Ship tab or the card button to send the tracking number the same day you drop it off.

Your action · Ship tab

4
Post-delivery follow-up

7 to 10 days after shipping, send a warm check-in. The dashboard will flag this when it's due. Builds loyalty and repeat buyers.

Your action · Follow-up tab

5
Retention

When new work drops, notify past buyers first. When a print sells out, collect waitlist names — then use the Waitlist tab to let them know if a print frees up, or if that photograph gets a new edition.

Your action · Waitlist tab

Image Protection

Three-layer defence against screenshots, sharpening, and AI upscaling.

Active layers
✓
55% resolution cap
Browser never receives the full-resolution image. Sharpening and upscaling can't recover pixels that were never sent.
✓
Temporal dithering
Canvas alternates pixel sets at 22 fps. A screenshot captures one frame - a soft, cross-hatched ghost of a 55%-res render.
✓
Steganographic fingerprint
Each visitor session gets a unique invisible watermark encoded into pixel LSBs. Leaked screenshots can be traced to the specific visitor.
◯
Adversarial AI poisoning
Bakes imperceptible pixel perturbations into source images. AI upscalers output your logo at 80% center instead of enhancing the photo.
