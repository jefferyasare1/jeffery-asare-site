Why email fonts work differently from the website

On the site itself, a browser loads whatever font you link to, no restrictions. Email is a different medium: Gmail, Outlook.com, and Yahoo Mail strip out @font-face rules from HTML email for consistency and security, regardless of whether the font file exists. Only Apple Mail, iOS Mail, and (partially) Outlook desktop honor custom fonts in email at all. That's an industry-wide limitation, not something specific to your setup.

So the missing file is one real bug worth fixing on its own — a small win for however many people read your emails in Apple Mail. But for the Gmail majority in your screenshots, the only lever that actually changes what they see is the type scale in Option 03.
